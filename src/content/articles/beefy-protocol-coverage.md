DeFi as an industry is slowly groiwng up. Users are moving from risk-tolerant yield chasing to low-risk, high-volume investments supported by trusted entities. But, despite this development, there are still various core components of financial systems which are broadly lacking across the industry.

In recent months, Staworth — along with the contributor teams of Beefy and Nexus — have been working to develop a plan to bring integrated protocol coverage to Beefy. That journey has raised many questions and led us down many rabbit holes. But the further we go, the more sense this makes.

In this essay, we argue that DeFi as an industry needs to progress towards integrated risk coverage if it is going to absorb the world's financial markets whilst remaining decentralised.

## For The Many, Not The Few

One key theme we’ve worked through for Beefy has been whether the benefits of protocol insurance should be made exclusive — limited to or prioritized for our tokenholders, given that it is the DAO that governs the protocol which is supporting and subsidising the work.

The justification is not just about funding, but goes to the heart of the user-holder-contributor dynamic, which most entities can recognise. Personally, I like the mental model of “scale economies shared” espoused by Sleep and Zakaria in their case for the competitive advantages of CostCo’s business model: the bigger Costco grows, the greater its scale advantages pass benefits on to all three groups. And all three groups are properly incentivized to pursue that growth in a manner that’s aligned with the others’ interests.

## For Beefy, And For Everyone

Beefy is particularly well suited to this model, because our TVL is diversified across a wide range of risks. Experience tells us that over a long enough time period there will be some incident that can benefit from cover. But experience also tells us that this will affect a smaller fraction of our TVL, meaning fractional coverage is entirely appropriate.

## That's Not What DeFi Is About

The more exotic yield options tend to crossover with those that won't be protected. Dedicated yield farmers willing to try out the latest chains and move quickly to soak up incentives will always have a place on Beefy. But so do set-it-and-forget-it investors who value safe, reliable yield over bottom-line returns.
