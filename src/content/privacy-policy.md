[Staworth Limited (UK Company No. 15540474)](https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/15540474) (**"Staworth"**) is a private limited company registered in England. We are committed to the proper and transparent handling of personal data, in line with our responsibilities under English Law.

This policy sets out how and the bases on which we process personal data, as well as what we collect, store and share, your rights in respect to your data, and the means of contacting us or raising complaints. 

It applies to all of Staworth's websites and digital services operated under our owned domains, including `staworth.com`, `staworth.co.uk`, and `staworth.org`. It also applies to any and all of their subdomains.

## Applicable Law

This Privacy Policy, and any dispute or claim arising out of or in connection with it, is governed by the laws of England and Wales. The courts of England and Wales shall have exclusive jurisdiction, subject to any mandatory rights you may have under applicable law.

Where we process personal data, this policy aims to align our practices with the following domestic legislation (without limitation) where relevant:

- The UK General Data Protection Regulation (**UK GDPR**);
- The Data Protection Act 2018 (**DPA**); and
- The Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations 2003 (**PECR**).

Though we will endeavour to ensure this policy is always kept up to date with the latest legislative requirements, we reaffirm our commitment to abide by all relevant requirements as soon as they become applicable, even if not yet explicitly mentioned or incorporated into this policy. We reserve the right to update or amend this policy as needed from time to time without notice. 

## What We Collect

When you contact us via [our email address](mailto:{{CONTACT_TO_EMAIL}}) or through our [Contact page](http://staworth.com/contact), we will collect:

- your email address;
- the subject line of your message; and
- the message content you choose to provide (including any personal data you add).

We aim to collect only the minimum personal data required to respond to your enquiry, and ask that you do not share excessive personal data or the personal data of other individuals that you are not responsible for without their consent.

This website and Staworth's services are not intended for children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

We do not use any personal data:

- to create intrusive behavioural profiles or carry out automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects;
- for any purpose that is incompatible with the reason it was originally collected; or
- in any way that is unlawful, unfair, misleading, or not transparent.

## Your Rights

If you are in the UK, you have rights under UK data protection law, including the right to:

- be informed about how your personal data is used
- request access to your personal data
- request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal data
- request erasure of your personal data in certain circumstances
- request restriction of processing in certain circumstances
- object to processing based on legitimate interests
- request portability of personal data you have provided to us, where applicable
- withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent
- request review of decisions based solely on automated processing, where applicable

To exercise these rights, contact us at [{{CONTACT_TO_EMAIL}}](mailto:{{CONTACT_TO_EMAIL}}). We may need to verify your identity before completing a request. We will usually respond within one month, though this may be extended where permitted by law.

You also have the right to make a complaint with the [Information Commissioner's Office (**ICO**)](https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/).
